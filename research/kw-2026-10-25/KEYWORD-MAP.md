## 3. Findings that change the plan

1. **Our own 12pointbolt.com/ms21250 page says "160 ksi" in its title.** That contradicts VERIFIED-FACTS (180 ksi). Search Console, 480 days: 6 impressions, position 33. The same 160 figure appears in NSN records (govscraper, nsnlookup), aft.systems and ChatGPT's answer.
   - **Action**: fix it in the content phase, and give the ms21250 site a section on why NSN records say 160 ksi. That section can only go in once the source of the 160 is verified; until then, publish 180 ksi only.
2. **The shear and strength wording in the SERPs varies** (NSN records "great shear stress", Monroe "shear bolt", the drawing "tension bolt"). This supports the planned shear stress vs per-bolt load page.
3. **Materials may be upgradeable.** The AI Overview quotes the aaestore drawing (S2) for the material list: MIL-S-5626 / 4140, MIL-S-5000 / 4340, MIL-S-6049 / 8740, MIL-S-8503 / 6150. VERIFIED-FACTS marks those grades ⚠️ (S5 only).
   - **Action**: re-read S2. If the drawing lists them, the materials page can name the grades.
4. **ms21250.com has no Search Console property** that the service account can read. Add one at build time: the owner verifies it, then adds the service account.
5. **12pointbolt.com overlap.** Pages that touch this topic: /ms21250, and /12-point-fasteners (which ranks 38.5 for "ms21250 spec"). Plan: when ms21250.com goes live, 301 12pointbolt.com/ms21250 to ms21250.com. It has close to zero traffic, so nothing is lost and the signal consolidates. The other 5 mentions get links. That is a live change, so it needs its own go-live.
6. **Existing repo page `ms21250-dimensions`.** The per-size dimension table is on the DO NOT PUBLISH list. Audit that page before reusing it.
