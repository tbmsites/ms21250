    MS21250 Bolts: 12-Point Tension Bolts

    High-Tensile Alloy Steel External Wrenching Bolts to MS21250 / NASM21250

    MS21250 is the Military Standard for a high-strength tension bolt: a flanged, 12-point (double-hex) external wrenching head bolt machined from alloy steel and rated to a minimum ultimate tensile strength of 180,000 psi (180 ksi). The standard was later redesignated NASM21250, with the dimensional and mechanical requirements carried over unchanged. TorqBolt manufactures and supplies bolts produced to MS21250 / NASM21250 requirements from our works in Mumbai and Rajkot, India, with full material traceability and inspection documentation.

    MS21250 at a glance

    An MS21250 bolt is identified by its flanged 12-point head, which lets a wrenching socket grip twelve bearing points instead of the six on a standard hex. That geometry transmits high installation torque without rounding the corners, so the fastener can be preloaded to the high tensions its 180 ksi material allows. The integral flange spreads the clamp load over a wider bearing area under the head. Bolts to this standard are used where a compact, high-strength threaded joint has to be torqued repeatedly or to a precise preload.

    The name itself is descriptive. The standard title reads "Bolt, Tension, Steel, External Wrenching, Flanged, 12-Point", and each phrase carries meaning: it is a tension bolt built to be loaded along its axis, made of steel, driven from the outside of the head rather than from an internal socket, fitted with a flange, and wrenched on twelve points. Because the drive is external, the head can be smaller across the flats than a comparable internal-drive fastener of the same strength, which is useful where wrench access is tight. The 12-point pattern also lets a tool engage the head at finer angular steps than a six-point hex, helping installation in confined spaces where a full wrench swing is not possible.

    Strength is the reason the standard exists. At a minimum ultimate tensile strength of 180 ksi, an MS21250 bolt sits well above a general-purpose commercial bolt, so a given diameter can carry more clamp load or a smaller diameter can do the same job. The alloy steel is heat treated to reach that level, and the bolt is rated for continuous service to 450°F. The combination of high strength, a wrenching head and a flanged bearing face is what makes the part suited to demanding, repeatedly torqued joints.

    Applications

    The 12-point tension bolt is chosen where joints carry high preload in a confined envelope and where the fastener may be torqued to a controlled tension. Typical uses include structural and machinery joints, engine and drivetrain assemblies, and any bolted connection that benefits from a wrenching head able to take repeated high-torque tightening. The optional drilled head (the H variant) accepts safety wire where a positive locking method is required.

    Two features drive those applications. The first is the head: twelve external wrenching points let a socket apply and re-apply high torque without distorting the corners, which matters on joints that are assembled and disassembled over a service life. The second is the strength class: at 180 ksi the bolt can be preloaded to a high tension, so the joint stays clamped under vibration and cyclic load instead of loosening. Where a joint must also resist accidental back-off, the drilled-head H variant lets the installer pass safety wire through the head and lock the bolt positively against rotation.

    Selecting the right part for an application comes down to three choices: the diameter dash code that sets the shank size, the grip length that matches the stack of parts being clamped, and whether a drilled head is required. Getting the grip right matters, because the unthreaded grip should span the joint so that the thread does not bear in the clamped material. The bolt chart lays out the dash and grip codes so a part number can be built from a known joint thickness.

    Where MS21250 sits

    MS21250 began life as a Military Standard sheet drawn against the procurement specification MIL-B-8831. When U.S. defense fastener standards were transferred to industry stewardship, MS21250 was redesignated NASM21250 and the requirements were retained. A buyer specifying any of these three callouts is asking for the same bolt; the differences are administrative rather than dimensional. The detail of that lineage is set out on the MIL-B-8831 page.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is an MS21250 bolt?

    MS21250 is a Military Standard for a flanged, 12-point external wrenching tension bolt made from alloy steel and rated to a minimum ultimate tensile strength of 180 ksi. The standard is now also known as NASM21250.

    Does TorqBolt supply MS21250 bolts?

    Yes. TorqBolt manufactures bolting to MS21250 / NASM21250 requirements under an ISO 9001 quality system, with PED 2014/68/EU and AD 2000-Merkblatt material capability and full heat-to-bolt traceability.

    Is MS21250 the same as NASM21250?

    Effectively yes. NASM21250 is the redesignation of MS21250; the dimensional and mechanical requirements were carried across unchanged, so the two callouts describe the same bolt.

    How do I order the right MS21250 part number?

    Quote the part number in the form MS21250[H]-DD-LLL: add H for a drilled head, choose the diameter dash code, and give the grip length in sixteenths of an inch. The bolt chart page decodes the dash and grip codes.
